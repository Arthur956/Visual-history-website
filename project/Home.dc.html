The problem

Ruins ask visitors for an imagination most of them can't supply.

01
A few stones, a sign, a floor plan.

Without help, most visitors walk past foundations without ever seeing the building that stood there.

02

03
Today's tools fall short.

How a reconstruction is built

Every stone starts with a historian.

Accuracy is what makes a reconstruction worth trusting. Each scene is researched with historians and validated before it reaches a visitor, and every source is documented.

  1. 01
    Historical research & validation

    Archives, excavation reports and expert review. Nothing is modelled before it is sourced.

    HISTORIAN-VALIDATED
  2. 02
    3D modelling

    Architecture, materials and period life built from the validated research.

  3. 03
    Spatial anchoring

    LiDAR scans of the site align the model to the real foundations.

  4. 04
    Optimisation & UX testing

    Tested on site with real visitors, including accessibility needs.

  5. 05
    Deployment & handover

    Installed, staff trained, and supported from day one.
