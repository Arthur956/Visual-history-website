Process · Visual History

Five stages. One standard of accuracy.

Historian validation is the currency of trust in heritage. It opens every project and is documented at every stage, so curators, boards and grant evaluators can see exactly what a reconstruction is based on.

  1. STAGE 01

    Historical research & validation

    HISTORIAN-VALIDATED

    We gather archives, excavation reports, comparable sites and period sources, then work with historians and your own curators to agree what can be shown with confidence and where interpretation is needed.

    YOU RECEIVEA source dossier and a validated reconstruction brief.
  2. STAGE 02

    3D modelling

    Architecture, materials, objects and period life are modelled from the brief. Uncertain elements are flagged so they can be shown honestly to visitors.

    YOU RECEIVEReview renders at each milestone.
  3. STAGE 03

    Spatial anchoring

    We LiDAR-scan the site and align the model to the real foundations at 1:1, so the past appears exactly where it stood.

    YOU RECEIVEA digital scan of your site, useful for preservation records.
  4. STAGE 04

    Optimisation & UX testing

    The experience is tuned for comfort and performance, then tested on site with real visitors, including people with visual, hearing and mobility needs.

    YOU RECEIVEA test report with visitor feedback.
  5. STAGE 05

    Deployment & handover

    We install hardware, train your staff and connect the CMS and analytics dashboard. Maintenance and content updates continue for the length of the licence.

    YOU RECEIVEA live experience, trained team and ongoing support.
