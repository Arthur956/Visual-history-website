One reconstruction. Two ways in.

Each historian-validated reconstruction can be delivered on site through mixed reality, or as an immersive VR journey anywhere. Many partners combine both.

On-site Mixed Reality

Visitors walk the real site wearing a lightweight headset. Walls, roofs and people reappear exactly where they stood, aligned to LiDAR scans of the remaining foundations.
